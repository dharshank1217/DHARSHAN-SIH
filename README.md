## Problem Title
**SIH 26033: Multiple Intermediaries Reduce Farmers' Earnings and Increase Consumer Prices**

## Problem Creator's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
In the current agricultural supply chain, farmers often depend on multiple intermediaries to sell their products. These intermediaries increase the final price of agricultural products while reducing the income received by farmers.

A digital marketplace is required to connect farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers. The platform should also provide logistics support and use Artificial Intelligence for demand forecasting and route optimization.

## Proposed Solution

**FarmerDirect – Smart Agricultural Marketplace**

FarmerDirect is an AI-powered digital marketplace that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers, restaurants and bulk buyers.

The platform allows farmers to upload their available products, quantities and prices. Buyers can browse products and purchase directly from farmers without depending on multiple intermediaries.

The system also provides transportation support and AI-based demand forecasting to help farmers understand market demand and sell their products efficiently.

**How it addresses the problem:**
- Reduces the number of intermediaries.
- Helps farmers receive better prices.
- Provides affordable agricultural products to consumers.
- Connects farmers with bulk buyers.
- Improves transportation and delivery management.

**Innovation and Uniqueness:**
- AI-based demand prediction.
- Smart price comparison between different markets.
- Direct farmer-to-consumer communication.
- GPS-based delivery and route optimization.
- Digital platform supporting both individual and bulk purchases.

**Technologies to be Used:**

- Frontend: React.js
- Backend: Python (FastAPI)
- Database: MySQL
- AI/ML: Python, Scikit-learn
- Maps and Location: Google Maps API
- Cloud Hosting: AWS
- Mobile Application: Flutter

**Methodology and Implementation:**

1. Develop a registration system for farmers and buyers.
2. Allow farmers to upload products, prices and quantities.
3. Develop a marketplace for browsing and purchasing products.
4. Integrate AI to forecast product demand.
5. Implement route optimization for transportation.
6. Provide order tracking and delivery updates.
7. Test the platform with sample farmer and buyer data.

**Basic Workflow:**

Farmer Registration → Product Upload → Digital Marketplace → Buyer Selection → Order Confirmation → Logistics Management → Product Delivery.

## Feasibility and Viability

**Feasibility:**

The proposed solution is technically feasible using existing web technologies, cloud services, AI algorithms and GPS-based mapping systems.

**Potential Challenges and Risks:**
- Limited digital knowledge among some farmers.
- Transportation and storage difficulties.
- Fluctuations in agricultural product prices.
- Maintaining product quality.
- Building trust between farmers and buyers.

**Strategies to Overcome Challenges:**
- Provide a simple and multilingual user interface.
- Partner with local transportation services.
- Introduce transparent pricing systems.
- Provide product quality information.
- Implement secure payment and order tracking systems.

## Impact and Benefits

**Impact on Target Audience:**

The platform mainly benefits farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), consumers, retailers, restaurants and wholesale buyers.

**Social Benefits:**
- Improves communication between farmers and consumers.
- Supports rural digital development.
- Creates better market opportunities for small farmers.

**Economic Benefits:**
- Increases farmers' income.
- Reduces unnecessary intermediary costs.
- Provides affordable products to consumers.
- Improves market accessibility.

**Environmental Benefits:**
- Reduces unnecessary transportation.
- Minimizes food wastage through demand forecasting.
- Improves delivery route efficiency.
- Supports sustainable agricultural distribution.
